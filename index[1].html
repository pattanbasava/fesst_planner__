DURING THE FEST

On-ground essentials

  • Keep registration and help desks visible.
  • Have a stage manager and timekeeper.
  • Maintain clear emergency access routes.
  • Use crowd guidance and queue management.
  • Track equipment issue and return.
  • Keep first aid and drinking water accessible.
These are common event-planning suggestions, not official DBIT rules. Follow the latest instructions issued by the college and event authorities.
